# Scholarly search entry points / 学术检索入口

Use this reference when choosing a publisher's official search route or an appropriate cross-publisher discovery service. These are examples, **not an exclusive list or a mandatory multi-site search plan**. Custom sources remain supported; identify an unlisted journal's official platform when needed.

## Official publisher routes

| Publisher / platform | Official entry point | Coverage and filtering notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IEEE / IEEE Xplore | [ieeexplore.ieee.org](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/) | Journals, conferences, standards and partner content. IEEE-Trans requires checking the exact journal, including joint Transactions; other Xplore content is not automatically eligible. |
| Elsevier / ScienceDirect | [sciencedirect.com](https://www.sciencedirect.com/) | Journal articles and book content. Apply the selected journal, date and article-type restrictions. |
| Springer Nature Link | [link.springer.com](https://link.springer.com/) | Journals, books, reference works and protocols. Check the actual publication and publisher rather than relying on the platform name. |
| Nature Portfolio | [nature.com](https://www.nature.com/) | Use the site's Search or the selected journal's search. Distinguish research articles from news, commentary and other content; some content overlaps with Springer Nature Link. |
| Wiley Online Library | [onlinelibrary.wiley.com](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/) | Journals, books and reference works. Confirm the selected journal, publisher and article type. |
| Taylor & Francis Online | [tandfonline.com](https://www.tandfonline.com/) | Journal articles. Apply the requested publication and date filters. |
| Sage Journals | [journals.sagepub.com](https://journals.sagepub.com/) | Journal content. Apply journal and article-type restrictions; distinguish research from editorial material. |
| ACM Digital Library | [dl.acm.org](https://dl.acm.org/) | ACM publications and broader bibliographic discovery content. The Guide to Computing Literature can include other publishers; verify publisher, venue and publication type. |
| Oxford Academic | [academic.oup.com](https://academic.oup.com/) | Journals and books. Use the selected journal's official page where appropriate and filter publication type. |
| Cambridge Core | [cambridge.org/core](https://www.cambridge.org/core) | Journals and books. Confirm the venue and content type rather than admitting the whole catalog. |

## Optional cross-publisher discovery

| Service | Entry point | Role and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Scholar | [scholar.google.com](https://scholar.google.com/) | Google's scholarly search service, not a publisher's official database. Useful for cross-publisher discovery, cited-by links and related articles; results can include theses, books, preprints and other material. Verify publication scope and bibliographic identifiers against the actual publisher record before saving. |

Choose this service when appropriate for Global scope, or as a user-approved supplementary discovery entry point for a restricted scope. Changing the search entry point does not widen the confirmed publication scope. Do not assume it offers complete coverage or easier automation: Google Scholar restricts automated bulk access. See its [official overview](https://scholar.google.com/intl/en/scholar/about.html) and [search/access help](https://scholar.google.com/intl/en/scholar/help.html). Record access limits and follow supported access methods.

## Use and maintenance

- Prefer the official platform's supported website search or an already configured official API. Home-page links are stable starting points, not undocumented API endpoints or fixed query templates.
- Check official identity and redirects at time of use, reusing current cached evidence where available. A valid official URL does not guarantee tool access, full-text access or complete retrieval.
- Use only routes that fit the confirmed scope. Global scope allows an appropriate scholarly database; this table does not require separate publisher passes. Follow the skill's user-choice rules before switching discovery entry points or widening publication scope.
- Deduplicate overlapping results and reuse verified bibliographic evidence. DOI registry lookups are single-record verification, not extra thematic searches.
